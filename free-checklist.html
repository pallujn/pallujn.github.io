The 7-Day Calm Dog Checklist

A simple starter week for everyday owners. One short habit a day. No perfect session required.

Get the free checklist

The form and download are hosted on Systeme.io, the tool we use to email the PDF and the short follow-up sequence. If the embed does not load, use the button above.

What’s inside

  1. The Five-Minute Settle — a mat your dog can relax on
  2. The Calm Hello — four paws on the floor before anyone pets
  3. The Leash Reset — the walk moves when the leash is loose
  4. The Quiet Check-In — a better plan than saying “no” to barking
  5. The Trade-Up — pay the better choice around counters and treasures
  6. The Name Game Outdoors — become interesting again when the world is loud
  7. A Job for the Brain — short sniffing games that take the edge off

Plus a weekly tracker you can put on the fridge.

This is a starter plan, not a complete training program, a diagnosis, or a guarantee. If you are worried about a bite risk or a situation you cannot manage safely, pause and get qualified in-person help.
