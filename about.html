About Calm Canine Daily

Calm Canine Daily is a small educational site for everyday US dog owners. We publish practical, reward-based tips on barking, jumping, leash manners, puppy skills, and settling at home — plus a free 7-Day Calm Dog Checklist.

The project is run by Jonathan Pereira. The voice of the site is the same one you may already know from our short videos: warm, calm, and specific, never shouty. We write as a helpful neighbor, not as a guru.

What we are — and what we are not

We are not a veterinary clinic, and we are not a certified dog-training practice. We do not claim CPDT, CBCC, or DVM credentials, and we do not diagnose medical or behavior conditions. Nothing on this site is a substitute for a veterinarian or for a qualified in-person trainer or behavior professional when a situation is unsafe, stuck, or over your head.

When we describe a training idea, we stick to methods we can explain plainly: invitation, management, and rewards. We do not use or recommend prong collars, shock, spray, or dominance/"alpha" framing.

The free checklist

Our primary tool is the 7-Day Calm Dog Checklist. It is a starter week, not a complete program. Each day is one short habit. You do not need a perfect session.

How we make recommendations

If we mention a paid program or, later, a piece of gear, we say so. Our featured program is Brain Training for Dogs, which we recommend as independent ClickBank affiliates. We are not the course creator, we do not speak for Adrienne Farricelli, and no trainer has endorsed our checklist just because we may link to their work. We do not invent testimonials, star ratings, or results.

Read the full Affiliate Disclosure and our Brain Training for Dogs review.

Editorial standards

  • No invented statistics, studies, or credentials.
  • Update dates on articles; we correct mistakes when we learn about them.
  • Clear language when a reader should call a vet or hire a certified professional (for example, a CPDT-KA or CBCC-KA trainer, or another qualified local expert).
  • English for a US audience.
