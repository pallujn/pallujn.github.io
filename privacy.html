Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy describes how Calm Canine Daily (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use https://pallujn.github.io, our emails, and our checklist opt-in. It is written in plain language for a US audience. It is not legal advice.

We do not currently have a public street address to list here. For privacy questions, use the contact page or email the public address once it is active (planned: hello@calmcaninedaily.com).

What we collect

Information you give us. If you request the 7-Day Calm Dog Checklist, you provide at least an email address and, if you choose, a first name. That form and the follow-up emails are processed by Systeme.io on our behalf. If you write to us, we receive whatever you include in the message.

Information collected automatically (once the site is live). We plan to use Google Analytics 4 and related Google tools (such as Search Console) to see anonymous or pseudonymous usage data: pages viewed, approximate location at a city or region level, device type, and how you arrived (including UTM parameters). We also use cookies or similar storage for cookie banners if required, to remember that you closed a popup, and — if you click an affiliate link — to help the network attribute a purchase.

Affiliate partners. If you click /go/brain-training.html, you are sent to a ClickBank hop link. ClickBank and the vendor may set their own cookies and collect information under their policies. We may see reports by tracking IDs (tid) that tell us which button was clicked, not your name.

Payments. We do not collect credit-card numbers on this site. Purchases of Brain Training for Dogs are handled by ClickBank and the vendor. Future Amazon purchases happen on Amazon.

How we use information

  • To send the checklist and the welcome emails you requested
  • To reply if you contact us
  • To understand which articles and buttons are useful (analytics and tid reports)
  • To comply with the law, prevent abuse, and maintain the site

We do not sell your email list. We do not use your checklist signup to enroll you in unrelated third-party newsletters.

Legal bases and your choices (high level)

If you are in a region with privacy statutes such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, and to opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising. Email us to make a request. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in every message. You can also use browser controls and, where offered, a cookie banner to limit analytics cookies.

Retention

We keep email-list records until you unsubscribe or we close the list, and we keep analytics for a limited period set in the analytics tool. We keep correspondence as long as it is needed to answer you or meet a legal duty.

Processors and third parties we rely on or may rely on

  • Systeme.io (checklist form, email delivery, and related hosting of the lead magnet)
  • Hostinger or another host, once the site is deployed (site hosting and logs)
  • Google (planned Analytics 4 and Search Console)
  • ClickBank (affiliate clicks and any resulting purchase)
  • ImprovMX or a similar mail forwarder for the domain mailbox
  • Later: Amazon Associates, and possibly an ads partner such as Ezoic or Mediavine — this policy will be updated before those are turned on

Each of those companies has its own privacy policy.

Children

This site is written for adults who own or care for dogs. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
