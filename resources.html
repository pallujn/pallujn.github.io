When to get in-person help

If you are facing a bite risk, aggression, or a situation you cannot manage safely, an article or an online course is not enough. Look for a veterinarian and a qualified trainer or behavior professional in your area (credentials such as CPDT-KA or CBCC-KA are one useful filter, not the only one).
